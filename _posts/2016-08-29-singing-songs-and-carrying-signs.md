<p>Evidently some dude&#8217;s protesting the national anthem.</p>
<p>Don&#8217;t know who he is, don&#8217;t care, and doesn&#8217;t matter whether he&#8217;s protesting poverty, race-based policing, gay marriage, immigration, or the lack of vegetarian options at McDonald&#8217;s. Protesting the national anthem, the pledge, and burning the flag are the exact kind of speech the first amendment was designed to protect &#8211; we force ourselves to be open to seemingly outrageous speech so that we can get past the &#8220;outrage&#8221; and figure out why the speaker feels the need to push so hard to be heard. Sometime&#8217;s it&#8217;s because the rest of us just aren&#8217;t terribly concerned about the speech &#8211; whether it&#8217;s the illuminati or HRC&#8217;s lizard-person insides &#8211; but sometimes it&#8217;s because we, terribly, just aren&#8217;t concerned.</p>
<p>Go 1st Amendment! Let my people speak.</p>
