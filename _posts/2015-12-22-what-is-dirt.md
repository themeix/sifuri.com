<p>What is dirt?</p>
<p>From this article on <a href="https://medium.com/weird/no-litter-no-memes-b7bf70791175#.8rnlauyum">kitty litter</a>, we learn that:</p>
<blockquote><p>it’s strip-mined dirt that is transported overland via combustion engines and ends up in landfill where it never biodegrades.</p></blockquote>
<p>But isn’t “dirt” pretty much the definition of biodegraded, past tense? I mean, what does compost, bodies, dead trees, and wet paper turn into? Dirt.</p>
