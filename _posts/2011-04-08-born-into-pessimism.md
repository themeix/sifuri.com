<p>Sometimes, life is too fatalistic for my tastes. It&#8217;s a fundamentally anti-American thing to say that we are too <a href="http://www.bakadesuyo.com/2011/04/what-predicts-how-optimistic-you-are-about-lo/">molded by the past</a>, that our destinies are rough-hewn before we have control of our own lives. Sad, too, to learn too late that this is also true of those around us.</p>
<p>Certainly, this isn&#8217;t determinism; but without even knowing what causes us to shape and shade our thinking, how much are we hamstrung by invisible ropes? I thought briefly that perhaps I should never read research again, but my feeling here are they same as they are with consumer DNA testing: learning where the risks are gives us power to choose.</p>
<p>Ignorance is submission. Knowledge is power. Action is dignity.</p>
