<p>We&#8217;re running a <a href="https://www.crowdspring.com/logo-design/900743_logo-for-asdworldcom-a-social-network-based-site-serving-families-with-autistic-children/">logo contest</a> on <a href="https://www.crowdspring.com/">crowdSPRING</a> for a social network-based site serving the ASD (autistic spectrum disorder) community.</p>
<p>Besides wanting to see a greater variety of designs from different people rather than a number of variations on a theme from one person, I wanted to see how the original &#8220;thoughtstorm.com&#8221; might have functioned. My friends know that the genesis of &#8220;thoughtstorm&#8221; for me was a play on &#8220;brainstorm&#8221; as part of my <a href="/why-i-work-to-prototype-ideas/">C2B business idea</a>.</p>
<p>So, please feel free to spread notice of the contest to folks who should design an entry. Also, you can vote on the entries yourselves at the <a href="https://www.crowdspring.com/logo-design/900743_logo-for-asdworldcom-a-social-network-based-site-serving-families-with-autistic-children/">project page</a>.</p>
<p>If there are designers who want to improve the project pro bono but don&#8217;t like crowdSPRING or others on principle, <a href="http://www.imetrick.com/">send me</a> a design (I obviously won&#8217;t use unless we agree to a deal), post a link here as a comment (and then everyone else may get to coment), or enter and you can agree to donate the money to <a href="http://www.reedacademy.org/">REED Academy</a>, <a href="http://www.alpinelearninggroup.org/">Alpine Learning Group</a>&#8216;s Outreach Program, or <a href="https://www.autismspeaks.org/">Autism Speaks</a>. If you agree to that and do it, I&#8217;ll match the donation myself.</p>
