<p>You&#8217;re fire, a black market bottle rocket bought from the back of a trunk from a Pedro&#8217;s &#8220;South of the Border&#8221; bag.</p>
<p>I&#8217;m hoping for instant girlfriend, the one who I meet and that&#8217;s just it &#8212; it starts and continues and grows and goes on and on. I don&#8217;t want to date and date, and since I haven&#8217;t found her yet, I&#8217;d sorta like to give the rest back. But that&#8217;s not how it works, is it? They don&#8217;t teach you that in kindergarten.</p>
<p>I&#8217;m a keeper for someone. So are you.</p>
