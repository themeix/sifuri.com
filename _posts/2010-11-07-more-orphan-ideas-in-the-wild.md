<p>Seth Godin recently reported on the shipping of two ipad apps triggered or inspired or at least encouraged by earlier posts of his. I&#8217;ve written <a href="/why-i-work-to-prototype-ideas/">before</a> how my own growing lists of orphan ideas were triggered by some great ideas that Seth posted some time ago, and <a href="/why-sharing-orphan-ideas-works/">every</a> time I see that someone else has taken action on a notion like this, it encourages me.</p>
<p>My day will come, I&#8217;m sure. (Of course, more people read Seth than me, but so what &#8212; that&#8217;s really not the whole reason I release these ideas into the wild myself.)</p>
<blockquote><p><a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2010/10/killer-apps-now-shipping-early-days.html">Killer apps now shipping (early days)</a><br />
You may have read about my proposed apps:</p>
<p>One made it easier to run and manage meetings, and the other made it easier to present a powerpoint-style slideshow on the iPad (but better).</p>
<p>I&#8217;m delighted to report that early versions of both have been built by loyal readers who read the posts and chose to take action.</p></blockquote>
<p>I&#8217;m looking forward to checking out the presentation tool &#8212; it looks like a good idea for some deskside briefs that I give often, such as <a href="http://www.thoughtstorm.com/use-a-deal-diagram-shape-template-to-capture-details/">deal structures</a> for <a href="http://rickcolosimo.com/tag/venture-capital/">startup financing</a> or other transactions.</p>
