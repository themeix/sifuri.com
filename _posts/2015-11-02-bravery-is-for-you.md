<p>&#8220;Bravery is for other people,&#8221; Seth Godin <a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2015/10/bravery-is-for-other-people.html">writes</a>. He talks about how that just ain&#8217;t true.</p>
<p>We&#8217;re supposed to make the choices that don&#8217;t require our kids, or someone else&#8217;s, to grow up to be Audie Murphy or Rosa Parks. We&#8217;re supposed to make a series of small, non-dramatic, slightly better for the world choices that eventually elide the dramatic from our lives &#8211; at least in terms of problems <em>we</em> created.</p>
