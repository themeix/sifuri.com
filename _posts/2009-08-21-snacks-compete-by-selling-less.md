I saw the 100-calorie snack packs (a section that is now surprisingly large for a segment that didn&#8217;t exist that long ago). I told my <a href="http://pamcolosimo.blogspot.co.uk/">wife</a> that someday we&#8217;d see a 99-calorie snack pack. In fact, not too long ago, we spotted something like <a href="http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B001P7AYQW?ie=UTF8&amp;tag=thoughtstorm&amp;linkCode=as2&amp;camp=1789&amp;creative=390957&amp;creativeASIN=B001P7AYQW">this product</a>.</p>
