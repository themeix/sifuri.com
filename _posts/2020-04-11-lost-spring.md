<p>This is something I came up with to express one aspect of how we’ve all put lives on hold in different ways.</p>
<p>Hey, let’s get together; let’s get a drink and meet;<br />
Hey, let’s get together; I really think you’re sweet.<br />
Hey we got together; had a meal and talked all night;<br />
Hey we got together; wrapped my arms around you to hold you tight.</p>
<p>Hey let’s get together, I’m crazy ‘bout you<br />
Hey let’s get together, wanna wrap my arms around you.<br />
Hey let’s be together, I’ll try anything with you<br />
Hey let’s be together, firefly squid got the best of you.</p>
<p>We didn’t know then<br />
It was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We didn’t know how<br />
It was gonna upside-down<br />
We didn’t know it was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We started out fast and we never slowed down.</p>
<p>The days and weeks I spent with you really blew my mind.<br />
My love for you broke through; I wanted you to be mine.<br />
Hey let’s stay together; this is something true.<br />
Hey let’s stay together; I wanna live my life with you.</p>
<p>We didn’t know then<br />
It was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We didn’t know how<br />
It was gonna upside-down<br />
We didn’t know it was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We held each other then but we didn’t see now.</p>
<p>[bridge]</p>
<p>What if we put our lives together? I could build my life around you.<br />
Hey let’s put our lives together; our families, me, and you.<br />
But the world got between us, slowed everything down. [spoken]<br />
Told us to stay apart, to stay in our towns. [spoken]<br />
I got sick and you got scared.<br />
Were the two of us beyond repair?</p>
<p>We didn’t know then<br />
It was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We didn’t know how<br />
It was gonna upside-down<br />
We didn’t know it was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We tried to hold on but we didn’t know how.</p>
<p>So we’re searching for that lost spring<br />
Even though they’re all lost that came before.<br />
I’m looking for you in our lost spring<br />
I think you’re there, waiting somehow.<br />
Searching for each other in that lost spring<br />
Don’t forget the one coming to your door.</p>
<p>We didn’t know then<br />
It was gonna be a lost spring<br />
We didn’t know then and we don’t know now.</p>

<p>Notes:<br />
I’m clearly a word guy, not a music guy. So as I got started, the melody from the Grateful Dead’s St. Stephen helped me give it a bit of structure to pull things together. A few days later, I think I realized that it’s a folksy, almost country, sound in feel.</p>
