<p>This <a title="Ice water diet" href="http://health.howstuffworks.com/wellness/diet-fitness/weight-loss/question447.htm">article</a> on the caloric math behind drinking ice water to lose weight reminded me of something I wrote a number of years ago.</p>
<p>My friend John was at Comedy Central doing on-air promos at the time, and they were running a contest for &#8220;<a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b00jm3ms">Absolutely Fabulous</a>,&#8221; looking for story ideas.</p>
<p>The idea I submitted was that the two stars take up eating ice as a fad diet, which would lend itself to any number of gags suitable for that show. But what I liked  most about the idea was that it wasn&#8217;t as silly as it sounded.</p>
<p>According to How Stuff Works, drinking 8 8-ounce glasses of ice water per day would burn a net 70 Calories per day. If, however, you had an equivalent amount of ice cubes (for the purposes of this comparison, it&#8217;s assumed that the ice cubes would be in your mouth rather than in a glass of water, since that would allow heat loss to the atmosphere), you would burn an additional 79.7 calories per gram of water/ice, or an additional 150 Calories per day for a total of about 220 Calories/day.</p>
<p>That&#8217;s equivalent to 1 pound of weight loss every 16 days for ice, vs. 1 pound every 50 days for the ice water-only diet. On an annual basis, the ice diet would lead to a loss of over 22 pounds vs. 7 pounds for ice water.</p>
<p>I haven&#8217;t had a chance to confirm  how many typical ice cubes are equivalent to 64 ounces of water (the ice would have about 9% greater volume), or how long it would take an ice cube to melt in your mouth. It might be that you&#8217;d have a lot of ice in your mouth all the time. UPDATE: 8 oz of water is about 14 smallish ice cubes, and my unscientific test was 3 minutes to full melt, with just a little chewing at the end to hit the 3 minute mark. So, 14 cubes x 8 glasses = 112 ice cubes x 3 minutes = 336 minutes, or about 5 1/2 hours a day with ice in your mouth.</p>
<p>That sounds crazy, but some people will go through a lot of effort to avoid exercising.</p>
