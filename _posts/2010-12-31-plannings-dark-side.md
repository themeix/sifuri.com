<p>One <a href="http://www.thoughtstorm.com/quote-planning-too-much/">more reason</a> being a great <a href="http://www.thoughtstorm.com/eisenhower-quote-on-planning/">planner</a> isn&#8217;t always so good:</p>
<blockquote><p>If only she’d known that finished is the new perfect. If only she’d known that planning is the gateway drug to procrastination.</p></blockquote>
<p>From <a href="http://www.hellomynameisblog.com/2010/12/6-ways-to-be-better-boss-to-yourself.html">Scott Ginsberg</a>.</p>
<p>Have you used detailed planning, as &#8220;legitimate&#8221; meta-work, to tell yourself you&#8217;ve been working while still not <a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2010/12/maybe-next-year.html">getting anything done</a> and <a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2010/12/yearinreview.html">shipped</a>?</p>
