<p>The next zero-based exercise tip takes no time and close to no effort. When riding the subway or a bus for a reasonable distance, stand rather than sit. Our bodies use 17% (I&#8217;ll find a reference soon) more calories standing rather than sitting. That may not be a lot, but for zero effort, it&#8217;s great!</p>
<p>I do this on the subway in NYC, which also means I don&#8217;t have to look for a seat ever, and on buses from the airport to the rental car counter, for example. There&#8217;s no need to be silly about it, though: if your feet hurt, take a seat. If you&#8217;re on the 1-hour busride commute, take a seat. (However, when it comes to getting on the bus and standing vs. waiting for the next one, I&#8217;ll stand for the hour and save the extra 15-20 minutes.</p>
<p>As I said, I&#8217;ll look for the references and calculate the calorie difference between the two.</p>
<p>This minor, almost insignificant, change is the heart of the zero-based series. Small changes on the margin are easy to make and add up over time. I don&#8217;t think about standing on the subway anymore; I just do it.</p>
