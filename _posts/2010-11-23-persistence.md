<p>&#8220;And hanging crooked above his desk is a cheap black frame that contains an inspirational quote that has been attributed to Calvin Coolidge. Under the heading PRESS ON, it reads:&#8221;</p>
<blockquote><p>Nothing in the world can take the place of persistence. Talent will not; nothing is more common than unsuccessful men with talent. Genius will not; unrewarded genius is almost a proverb. Education alone will not; the world is full of educated derelicts. Persistence and determination alone are omnipotent.</p></blockquote>
<p>From an article re: the fellow who invented the Super Soaker. Takeaway: got to get my ass in gear. Too many smart thoughts, even some really, really great ones; I&#8217;m not a professor, getting paid to sit at a big wooden desk and come up with ideas. If the &#8220;idea man&#8221; ever existed in US commerce, he doesn&#8217;t today. And even then, &#8220;unrewarded genius&#8221; is no claim to fame.</p>
<p>Got to ship. (I&#8217;m not sure that shipping blog posts counts, but it&#8217;s part of the process as long as they get written and posted.)</p>
