<blockquote><p>Oatmeal with blueberries, fresh off the vine. The wrongest metaphor is pearls before swine.</p>
<p>A stick of cinnamon in black coffee I see. Can I lick the raspberry jam off of your cheek?</p>
<p>Tell me you miss me pretty please, and that someday you won&#8217;t cut me off at the knees.</p>
<p>I&#8217;m miles away in a cold dark room, sending you food &#8217;cause it&#8217;s all I can do.</p>
<p>I&#8217;ll see you again someday I&#8217;m quite sure. And I promise our breakfast won&#8217;t be so pure.</p></blockquote>
<p>-1/6/2011 (to be sung in a voice and rhythm of Bob Dylan)</p>
<p>So this poem/song/Dylan-inspired riff rolled off my tongue today. Here it is unedited.</p>
<p>Yes, it&#8217;s for you.</p>
