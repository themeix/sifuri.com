<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;">Someone once told me that she hadn&#8217;t seen me in a bad mood. (It was after only four weeks of knowing me, so maybe there&#8217;s a sample size problem.)</span></p>
<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767; min-height: 15.0px;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;"> </span></p>
<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767; min-height: 15.0px;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;">I said that something had annoyed me the week before. Things still bother me, but not for long and not as much. Remembering things I used to know about how I think about, approach, and deal with the world.</span></p>
<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767; min-height: 15.0px;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;"> </span></p>
<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;">In fact, as I write this&#8230;</span></p>
<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767; min-height: 15.0px;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;"> </span></p>
<p style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px; font: 12.0px 'Lucida Grande'; color: #676767;"><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;">I realize it&#8217;s not even that things bother me &#8212; it only bothers me when I&#8217;m not sure about my solution or course of action. Everything that is, just is. No being bothered by all of that: it&#8217;s not helpful.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Helvetica; font-size: 12px;">I can feel the changes in me when something else is stressing me &#8212; that&#8217;s what leads to my less, um, constructive reactions to otherwise meaningless things. So, I focus my efforts on dissociating the two: divide and conquer. In fact, I think that recognizing and naming the stress makes it easier </span></p>
