<h2 style="text-align: center;">#3</h2>
<p>This vase doesn&#8217;t belong here. These flowers should be near you, instead of still with me.</p>
<p>I wanted to plant the wine bottle in your apartment like a magic bean, sprouting lush growth, clearing the air all day in your absence.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>I walked out of the bar, clumps of frothy crystal slipping and skidding to the ground, losing altitude. Each one headed for a crash.</p>
<p>I turned down the streets, left, two straights, another left. I became a refuge for the flakes, a traveling airstrip.</p>
<p>There&#8217;s no yellow tape around us, no CNN, no NTSB to issue the report: Near miss? Minor collision? Pilot error?</p>
<p>Five words to you, five weeks ago, would have changed this poem.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&#8212; ca. 2011</p>
