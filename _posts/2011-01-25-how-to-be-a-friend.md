<p>Seth wrote a nice post called &#8220;<a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2011/01/ive-got-your-back.html">I&#8217;ve got your back</a>.&#8221; It reminded me instantly of two very different posts I&#8217;ve written that are about the same thing: <a href="http://www.thoughtstorm.com/why-do-you-need-a-ranger-buddy/">needing a ranger buddy</a> and <a href="/character-is-for-when-it-counts/">character</a>.</p>
<p>What do all of these have in common? On the one hand, they&#8217;re about trusting someone because that person is trustworthy and you know that you can rely on what you&#8217;ve been asked to rely on. On the other hand, they&#8217;re about the idea that pushing the envelope, taking a shot, reaching higher than your grasp, are all really goddamn hard sometimes.</p>
<p>Life is hard; in many places in the world it is still nasty, brutish, and short. In other places, we&#8217;ve traded one set of hardships for another (although plenty would be willing to trade places and call us whiners). It&#8217;s not easy to strike out on your own just because you have food to eat and central air; indeed, the golden handcuffs can make it harder, not easier. Seth can tell you about the <a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2010/01/quieting-the-lizard-brain.html">lizard brain</a> all day long to help you understand.</p>
<p>A ranger buddy can pull you up the mountain when that next step feels huge; a friend can help you find the confidence in yourself; a partner can love you whether you fail or succeed.</p>
<p>The hidden lesson here is for the supporters: you have to be as committed as the person you&#8217;re supporting. Fair weather friends, gold diggers (to use the vernacular) and spotlight rangers are worse to have in your life than not &#8212; they distract the doers, lure them into safety and then &#8211; WHAM &#8211; someone feels like they&#8217;ve been deceived and led into a trap. This isn&#8217;t just about starting businesses and creative endeavors: it&#8217;s about what &#8220;being there&#8221; for someone really means, the responsibility you have to actually be there, and what happens when you&#8217;ve decided to go and didn&#8217;t tell anyone. There&#8217;s no real difference in the potential for damage between walking away from a rock climber&#8217;s belay and hiding your true feelings; the one leaves prompt visible scars and the other takes its time and grows like a tumor.</p>
<p>Not one of us, not one, is enlarged by making another&#8217;s life small. And sometimes not hurting is the best help of all.</p>
