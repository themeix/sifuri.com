<p>I wrote this a while ago while describing my transition to the new Airbook (aka Macbook Air) and some new software, all of which necessitated and facilitated some significant changes in workflow.</p>
<blockquote><p>I really want to write a lot, lot more. I feel like it&#8217;s the crowbar I can use to open my future.</p></blockquote>
<p>What advice would I give someone who said this to me? I&#8217;m not really sure. Something I read earlier today was this line as a repetitive inquiry to get to fundamental needs/desires: &#8220;And if you had that, then what?&#8221;</p>
<p>So if I was writing a lot, then what? People would read it, I would make money doing it, I like sharing ideas, it doesn&#8217;t seem like work to write stuff (as in not boring-drudgery-uninspiring work), I could rearrange my schedule more to spend time with my boys and <a href="http://www.watchricktrain.com/">train for the Ironman</a>. Hmm. I think that&#8217;s a good start in terms of giving me some things to ponder.</p>
