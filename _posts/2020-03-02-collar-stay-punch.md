<p>I saw this <a href="https://www.uncommongoods.com/product/pick-punch">guitar pick tool</a> in the Uncommon Goods website some time ago. Around that time, I had the idea (since I don’t play guitar) of a similar tool that would punch out collar stays for dress shirts. However, between not wearing as many formal dress shirts at work and life, I never really followed through with my manufacturing contact friend.<br />
<img decoding="async" src="https://www.uncommongoods.com/images/items/20900/20926_2_640px.jpg" alt="Pick Punch"></p>
<p>Now, though, someone has caught up to me (my fault for not publishing this years ago, I suppose).</p>
<p>Behold, the <a href="https://methodshop.com/2018/10/pick-punch-collar-stay-punch.shtml">collar stay punch</a>!<br />
<img decoding="async" src="https://methodshop.com/wp-content/uploads/pick-punch-cards-1392x1044.jpg" alt="Pick Punch Collar Stay Punch"></p>
<p>Someone’s gone further and turned it into deliberate marketing at <a href="https://collarcard.com/">CollarCard</a>, where they pre-print the card with your design and prep the collar stays so they can be popped out.</p>
<p>I guess we need another use for all those hotel key cards now….</p>
