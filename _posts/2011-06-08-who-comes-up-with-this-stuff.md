<p>Here&#8217;s the Netflix blurb for a movie called &#8220;<a href="https://www.netflix.com/title/70120143">The Switch</a>:&#8221;</p>
<blockquote><p>Still single and increasingly attuned to the cacophony of her biological clock, 40-something Kassie (Jennifer Aniston) decides that if she can&#8217;t find a mate she&#8217;ll still pursue motherhood with the help of a sperm sample that&#8217;s not quite as anonymous as she thinks. As the baby grows up, Kassie&#8217;s best friend, Wally (Jason Bateman), agonizes over whether to reveal that he secretly replaced the donor sample with his own DNA.</p></blockquote>
<p>Is it me? Notwithstanding the Law &amp; Order SVU episode on a similar plot line, isn&#8217;t this rape? Wouldn&#8217;t any real woman think of it that way, even if it&#8217;s apparently <a href="http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2009/11/13/dr-ben-ramaley-fertility_n_357611.html">A-ok in Connecticut</a>?  (Yeah, I too pretty much threw up in my mouth a bit after reading that.) I mean, it&#8217;s as jacked up as I can imagine, and this movie is branded a comedy.</p>
<p>I guess I&#8217;ll follow up after I see how it turns out.</p>
