<p>Starbucks changes the rules again.</p>
<p>The new Starbucks reward program oddly mixes even and odd numbers to complicate the rewards system.</p>
<p>The original program (that is, the first program I participated in), gave one star per drink purchase. Since I often arrive with my kids and get them soy milk, that worked well for me. Three drinks =&gt; three stars.</p>
<p>The next program, the one that is going away in April, changed that rule to 1 star per purchase, meaning I got 1/3 the stars on those occasions, and even fewer when they got a snack. (My thin as a rail older son likes the double chocolate chunk brownies, and he can use the calories.) So one drink or three drinks or three drinks and two snacks =&gt; 1 star.</p>
<p>Here’s one view on the math (via <a href="http://fivethirtyeight.com/features/significant-digits-for-tuesday-feb-23-2016/">http://fivethirtyeight.com/features/significant-digits-for-tuesday-feb-23-2016/</a>):</p>
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-lang="en">
<p dir="ltr" lang="en">If you buy a <a href="https://twitter.com/search?q=%24SBUX&amp;src=ctag">$SBUX</a> coffee for $2.50, the old system gave you a free reward after 12 coffees; the new system after 25. 50+% devaluation!</p>
<p>— Josh Zumbrun (@JoshZumbrun) <a href="https://twitter.com/JoshZumbrun/status/701901990176100353">February 22, 2016</a></p></blockquote>
<p><script src="//platform.twitter.com/widgets.js" async="" charset="utf-8"></script></p>
<p>But here’s my scenario:</p>
<ul>
<li>New plan on its face: $62.50 = 125 stars = 1 reward.</li>
<li>Old – $2.62/venti tea * 12 = $31.44 for reward (I get a refill on my tea when I’m at the store; usually just one refill and then new bags. That $0.50 is actually free, so in some ways I’m getting ~18–24 teas for that $31.44 plus the reward, which I tend to use for a higher-priced item my kids get.)</li>
<li>New – $2.62/venti tea *  2 stars/$1 = 5 stars divided into 125 stars = 25 teas = $65.50 per reward.</li>
</ul>

<p>It remains to be seen where this will shake out, but the Starbucks app does have a history function that tracks both my $25 reloads and my earned rewards. It should be easy for me to see how this shakes out, provided I capture some numbers now. I suppose that tells me what my next post should be!</p>
