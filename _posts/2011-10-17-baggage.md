<p>Moved. Put all the &#8220;stuff&#8221; in one big pile. When the pile is gone, I&#8217;ll know I&#8217;m done decluttering.</p>
<p>Also, I was going to put my 8 or 10 sets of shelves (plastic, 6&#8242; high. 18&#8243; deep) out at the curb, rather than break them down and put them in my storage unit (whole &#8216;nother post needed on that one). They&#8217;ve been in the basement, mostly holding the stuff that&#8217;s in the other picture. I figured: No sense keeping them!</p>
<p>I realized that moving the shelves to the storage, knowing I can&#8217;t use them for at least a year, wasn&#8217;t worth the effort and time!</p>
<p>[Went to Home Depot to get keys made.]</p>
<p>I began rethinking my &#8220;give-away of the shelves! At Home Depot, they&#8217;re $40 each. I&#8217;ve got at least 10 downstairs, I figure. Seems like Craigslist after all!!!</p>
<p>One way to look at it is &#8220;Cool! I can sell those and get a couple hundred bucks&#8221; The other is that I had so much crap that I needed to buy $500 of shelves to put it in. When you think of it that way &#8212; was I just freaking blind all these years? I guess most of America was, so I don&#8217;t feel so bad: at least I&#8217;ve changed!</p>
