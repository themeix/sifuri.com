<p>I just read this insanely depressing quote from Ted Turner (yes, *that* Ted Turner, the BILLIONAIRE). He&#8217;s describing his three marriages ending in divorce, after the article discusses Ted&#8217;s weekly rotating girlfriends (four in total):</p>
<blockquote><p>Is he lonely? He pauses. &#8220;Maybe a little.”</p></blockquote>
<p>Yep, what&#8217;s the point of the billions if you&#8217;re still lonely? And do four women who are happy to be away from you for three weeks in a row really seem likely to fill that void?</p>
<p>I&#8217;m sorry, Ted. I&#8217;m looking for a pretty sweet wife who never makes me feel lonely. I&#8217;ll keep looking. Maybe before I get to your age I&#8217;ll decide to settle for three out of four big characteristics, but I&#8217;m nowhere close to giving up on love. Surrender is not a Ranger word.</p>
