<p>I often write in a prolific burst when starting a post, rushing to get the thoughts out of my head and onto the screen. But, in looking back over many old posts at <a href="http://www.thoughtstorm.com/">ThoughtStorm</a>  or <a href="http://rickcolosimo.com">rickcolosimo.com</a>, I see that I have sometimes, or often, failed to edit them as well as I could have.</p>
<blockquote><p>
The bad expositor may, and often does, provide an impressive volume of published work. It may contain a valuable record of profound thinking. But yet it will fail to be very effective.
</p></blockquote>
<p>&#8211;Reginald O. Kapp, The Presentation of Technical Information 5 (1948; repr. 1957). </p>
<p>So here&#8217;s to a new habit for a new year!</p>
