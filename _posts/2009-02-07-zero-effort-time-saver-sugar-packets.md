<p>The &#8220;zero-effort&#8221; time-saver series is a collection of short tips that I actually use to save time and be more productive. The key is things that are actually useful, cost basically nothing, and don&#8217;t take huge amounts of effort to implement.</p>
<p>First up: tear your sugar packets along the long side rather than the short side. The sugar dumps out more rapidly, which is the big time lag with sugar. If you drink 4 cups of a tea when spending a Saturday morning in Starbucks like I do, you might save a minute or so.</p>
<p>What&#8217;s a minute? Well, if I do that every week, it&#8217;s an hour. And, if it&#8217;s wasted, what&#8217;s the point of that? I reject the framing of the issue that says I have to justify saving a minute (again, with no effort and no cost); I say &#8220;why should I waste that minute?&#8221;</p>
