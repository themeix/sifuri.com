<h3>Public works from Triiibes.com</h3>
<p>The networking site <a href="http://triiibes.com/main/authorization/signIn?target=http%3A%2F%2Ftriiibes.com%2F">triiibes.com</a> was created by <a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/">Seth Godin</a>. It was an amazing experiment, and I&#8217;m very happy I participated. One rule of triiibes that made it more invigorating was that everything inside triiibes was to remain private.</p>
<p>That said, different groups of triiibesters collaborated to make a number of projects that were released to the public. (And I&#8217;m certain that many smaller collaborations and commenting happened, too.)</p>
