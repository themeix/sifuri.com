<p>Here are two related quotes that resurfaced in my life today (well, one did and that always makes me think of the second):</p>
<blockquote><p>Never attribute to malice that which can be adequately explained by stupidity.</p></blockquote>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanlon%27s_razor">-Hanlon&#8217;s Razor</a></p>
<p>The related quote tracks the famous <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufficiently_advanced_technology">Clarke&#8217;s [Third] Law</a>:</p>
<blockquote><p>Sufficiently advanced stupidity is indistinguishable from malice.</p></blockquote>
<p><a href="http://wikidumper.blogspot.co.uk/2007/07/greys-law.html">-Grey&#8217;s Law</a></p>
<p>I love science fiction authors. This stuff never shows up in the literary section.</p>
