<p>This fancy <a href="http://www.springwise.com/upbox/">meal in a box</a> gives me an interesting idea about how to launch the idea in a simpler version. Rather than go whole-hog to the full meal preparation and individualized diet =&gt; menu route, you could streamline the development and start with a launch of semi-specialized menus, such as heart-healthy, cancer risk, or longevity.</p>
<p>It would still be evidence-based but not personalized. It shouldn&#8217;t be too complicated to create some sort of scoring mechanism or other way to allow customers to keep track of the total nutrients they&#8217;ve ingested and even recommend next best choices for achieving the health goals of the customer.</p>
<p>I&#8217;ve got another post to come on this; the attention makes me wonder if I&#8217;m more interested in this idea than I thought.</p>
