<p>I was at the Go The Distance bike event, shortly after conquering the crappy feelings that glommed on to last year&#8217;s event. This year, I participated and biked 50 miles. It was literally my sixth ride on a road bike. Ever.</p>
<p>I was walking back to the car to get something for the boys, and I looked at the hundreds of people all around me.</p>
<blockquote><p>My kids are here, but now that my family&#8217;s been fractured, I feel alone, a stranger in my own life.</p></blockquote>
<p>I suppose if I were French and a philosopher, this would become the genesis of an existential novel. Instead, I&#8217;m an American and a Ranger and so I just said, &#8220;Hmmm,&#8221; and got on with it. Living, that is.</p>
<p>After all, Walker Percy is right. You can either just walk away, the pseudo-suicide, or keep at it. I love my kids; I&#8217;m keeping at it.</p>
