<p>Just posted this discussion to MacJournal&#8217;s forum, following up on some other discussions that tell me the world just hasn&#8217;t done what I want:</p>
<blockquote><p>Hi, I&#8217;m investigating MacJournal to allow me to videoblog simply directly from my mac/isight.</p>
<p>The video instructions seems to be missing the middle part, i.e., everything after record &amp; stop and before publish to my self-hosted WP installation.</p>
<p>is there a tutorial I&#8217;m not finding or an extra chapter to the manual? Because right now, I see the value in terms of journal entries for my own purposes, but getting those videos on to my blog seems to be a secret. And I&#8217;m unclear as to why the manual doesn&#8217;t seem to even acknowledge this.</p>
<p>FYI: I will be shooting all my own video, not posting links to youtube stuff or other found sources.</p>
<p>I am going to muddle through and see what happens if I just try it (i.e., the default way of doing things when we can&#8217;t find the instructions, at least in my family 🙂</p></blockquote>
<p>Here&#8217;s what I&#8217;m looking for:</p>
<blockquote><p>So I have a shiny new Mac (my first) with its nifty iSight/facetime  camera, and I&#8217;m looking for advice on how best to incorporate video  blogging into my WordPress self-hosted sites. I&#8217;d like to aim for  something that would be good for daily posting. All of the video will be  on one site for the time being, and I have a preference for hosting and  controlling my own files (rather than just posting them to  Youtube/vimeo by default).</p>
<p>The ability to shoot and use iPhone video (i4 w/ front camera) in the same general workflow would be a great bonus.</p></blockquote>
<p>and a slightly more detailed description of the process I see in my head:</p>
<blockquote><p>my thought is this:</p>
<p>I start &#8220;vblog.app&#8221;<br />
I hit the big green button that starts my camera and starts capturing from my camera.<br />
Big green button turns red.<br />
I say smart things.<br />
I push the big red button that stops the camera.<br />
[All the optional editing goes here, but for my initial plan, that&#8217;s not strictly necessary.]<br />
I push the big [WP blue] button that says publish.<br />
I fill out the dialog box with a post title, any add&#8217;l text, tags, and category (all as I would in WP).<br />
I push the big button that says publish.</p>
<p>At this point, everything else automagically happens and a post shows up on my site.</p>
<p>If I have to set prefs for video size, format, credentials for WP, and a  third-party site (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo, etc.), that could all be done  in advance so the process starts w/o me. Or so I would hope.</p>
<p>If the complicated part is editing, I&#8217;m talking about cutting that out (advisable or not is a separate question).</p>
<p>I guess another analogy is if I hit the little picture icon on my iphone  facebook app, it lets me access the camera or video camera, take a  pic/video, and upload. I recognize that the answer may be that FB does  all those intermediate steps automatically themselves, but then it&#8217;s no  longer a question of feasibility but probably coordination/APIs. (Cf. <a rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" href="http://www.cellspin.net/" target="_blank">http://www.cellspin.net/</a>,  which is an app tied to a webservice. And again, MacJournal claims to  support video blogging, but I haven&#8217;t tried it out yet. Probably  tomorrow!)</p></blockquote>
<p>I&#8217;m used to being called nuts for thinking of how pieces ought to work as a system; it&#8217;s <a href="http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2009/03/whats-your-super-power.html">my superpower</a>, as Seth Godin once asked. But this is what happens to me whenever I hit a speedbump of some kind: my mind starts racing, churning through all my experiences and the stuff I&#8217;ve read over the decades (yikes!) and the things I&#8217;ve learned how to figure out (like user experience, failure modes, and detailed planning) and out comes some reasonable version of what might work.</p>
<p>On a separate note, I need to find more and better ways of using my superpower. I need more clients with big thorny problems that need elegant and workable solutions.</p>
