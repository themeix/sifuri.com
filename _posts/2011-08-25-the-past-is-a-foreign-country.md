<p>It&#8217;s like I had an imaginary friend for the last 16 years and just found out she wasn&#8217;t real. All my memories are tainted, suspect. Who knows what was real and what wasn&#8217;t?</p>
<p>And I could drive myself nuts trying to figure it out, unearthing bits of evidence &#8212; photographs, tickets stubs, and snippets of half-remembered conversations. But what&#8217;s the point? At the end of The Sixth Sense, Bruce Willis is still dead; I&#8217;m still alive.<br />
How much of the past even matters?</p>
<p>We create our futures moment by moment starting now. Maybe the rest is just illusion, hallucination. Maybe there&#8217;s a reason I liked Jacob&#8217;s Ladder so much. </p>
<p>Maybe there&#8217;s a novel in here or at least a short story.</p>
